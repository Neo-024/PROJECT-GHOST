# NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 — Project Submission Draft

## Project title
PROJECT GHOST — Galactic History of Our Silent Travelers

## Summary
Project GHOST is an interactive 3D space-archaeology archive that helps young explorers discover the robotic and crewed hardware humanity has left on the Moon, Mars, and farther into the Solar System. Visitors navigate a schematic solar system, browse mission artifacts, and explore each object's purpose, scientific legacy, mission status, and documented location.

## Challenge connection
The project responds to "Abandoned but not Forgotten" by turning mission records into an accessible, exploratory learning experience. It distinguishes hardware left on a surface from spacecraft that were intentionally disposed of, atmospheric probes, and spacecraft that are still operating.

## What we built
- 3D interactive solar-system navigation scene.
- Destination and mission-status filters.
- Artifact biographies and mission records.
- Primary-source links and uncertainty notes.
- Local Archive Guide that answers from the curated record set.
