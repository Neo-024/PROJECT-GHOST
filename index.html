05 / WHY THIS ARCHIVE EXISTS

Not discarded.
Remembered.

Space exploration is more than the missions that return home. Hardware left on other worlds records engineering decisions, scientific breakthroughs, and the reach of human imagination. Project GHOST makes those stories discoverable for the next generation.

SCIENCE & SOURCE INTEGRITYMission status and location descriptions are simplified for learning. This prototype does not claim precise landing coordinates. Every record should be checked against the official challenge resource list and primary mission sources before final submission.
